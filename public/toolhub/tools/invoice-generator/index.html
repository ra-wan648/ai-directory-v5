How to use Invoice Generator

  1. Fill in the invoice details — Enter your business name, the client name, invoice number, dates and pick a currency.
  2. Add line items — Add one row per service or product with a description, quantity and rate — the preview totals update as you type.
  3. Download or print — Hit Download PDF for a real PDF file, or Print to use a paper copy. Your data never leaves your device.

What is Invoice Generator?

The Invoice Generator builds clean, professional invoices right in your browser: add unlimited line items, apply tax and discounts, and see an exact live preview of the document you will send.

How this utilitie tool works

Everything is computed instantly with plain JavaScript as you type, and the Download PDF button renders a real multi-page PDF using the pdf-lib library — no server round-trip, no signup, no watermark.

Privacy and limits

All form data lives only in the page's memory. Close the tab and it is gone — save the PDF to keep your copy. Very long invoices are split across multiple PDF pages automatically.

Invoice Generator — frequently asked questions

Is the invoice generator free to use?

Yes. The Invoice Generator on AI Directory is completely free — no account, no watermark, and no limit on how many invoices you create.

Does my invoice data leave my browser?

No. The live preview and the PDF are both generated locally in your browser with JavaScript (the PDF uses the pdf-lib library). Your client and business details are never uploaded anywhere.

Which currencies are supported?

USD, EUR, GBP, BDT, INR, PKR, AUD, CAD, AED and SAR. The symbol you pick is used in the preview and the PDF.

Can I edit the invoice after downloading the PDF?

Yes — keep the page open, change any field or line item, and the preview updates instantly. Just hit Download PDF again. Nothing is saved when you close the tab, so keep your own copy of the PDF.
