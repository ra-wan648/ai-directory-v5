How to use Flashcard Maker

  1. Add cards — Type a question or term on the front and the answer on the back, then press Add card. Your deck saves automatically in the browser.
  2. Study the deck — Open Study mode, tap the card to flip it, and mark each card Known or Unknown after answering.
  3. Track and export — Watch the progress bar and score, shuffle for a fresh order, and export your deck as JSON to back it up.

What is Flashcard Maker?

The Flashcard Maker is a no-frills study tool: build a deck of question/answer cards, then drill yourself in study mode with a 3D flip animation, shuffle, and known/unknown self-scoring.

How this education tool works

Cards are stored in your browser's localStorage, so your deck survives reloads. Study mode walks the deck in order (or shuffled), the progress bar tracks your position, and the score shows the share of cards you marked Known this session.

Privacy and limits

Decks live only on this device — nothing is uploaded or synced. Export JSON regularly if your cards matter, and note that clearing browser storage deletes the deck.

Flashcard Maker — frequently asked questions

Where are my flashcards saved?

In your browser's localStorage on this device — they survive page reloads and browser restarts, but they are not synced anywhere. Use Export JSON to back up or move a deck.

How does the study mode scoring work?

After flipping a card you mark it Known or Unknown. Your score is the share of Known cards in the current session, and the progress bar shows how far through the deck you are.

Can I import flashcards from a file?

Yes — export produces a JSON file like [{“front”:“...”,“back”:“...”}]. Use Import to load that file back, or to load a deck made elsewhere in the same format.

Is the flashcard maker free?

Yes — free, unlimited cards, no account and no signup. Everything runs locally in your browser.
