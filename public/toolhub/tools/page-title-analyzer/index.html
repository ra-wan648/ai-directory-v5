What is the Page Title Analyzer?

The title tag is the single most important on-page SEO element. This tool scores yours from 0 to 100 on length, pixel width, keyword placement, capitalization, word count and emotional pull — with concrete suggestions for every failed check.

Why pixel width beats character count

Google truncates titles at roughly 600 pixels, not 60 characters — a title full of wide capitals can be cut off at 45 characters. The tool measures your title with the canvas API at Google's real title font so the width reading matches what searchers see.

Anatomy of a strong title

Front-load your main keyword, keep it 50–60 characters, use one power word for click appeal, write in sentence case, and end with a short brand name after a separator if you have room.

Page Title Analyzer — frequently asked questions

Is the Page Title Analyzer free?

Yes — free with no signup and no limits.

What is a good title score?

Aim for 80+. Scores of 60–79 are workable but have clear fixes; below 60 the title is likely too long, too short, or missing its keyword.

What are power words?

Emotion- and action-driven words like free, proven, ultimate, exclusive or guaranteed that lift click-through rates when used honestly.

Does this guarantee better rankings?

No tool can promise rankings, but a well-optimized title improves both relevance signals and click-through rate, which is the part of SEO you fully control.
