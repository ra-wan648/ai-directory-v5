How to use

  1. Set your lengths — Keep the classic 25-minute focus and 5-minute break, or dial in your own rhythm.
  2. Start the timer — Press Start and work on one thing. The ring counts down, and a browser notification fires at each phase change.
  3. Build your streak — Finished focus sessions are saved as today's count in your browser, with dots for your recent sessions.

What is the Pomodoro Timer?

The Pomodoro Timer applies the classic Pomodoro technique: focused work in 25-minute blocks separated by 5-minute breaks. After four focus sessions, take a longer rest. This page gives you a big countdown ring, start/pause/reset/skip controls, session dots, and a daily counter so you can see how many deep-work blocks you actually completed.

How this education tool works

The timer runs on timestamps rather than tick counts, so it stays accurate even if the tab sits in the background. Phase changes trigger a browser notification (with your permission), and your completed sessions are stored in your browser's local storage — no account, no server, and the count resets automatically each new day.

Pomodoro Timer — frequently asked questions

Is the Pomodoro Timer free to use?

Yes. Unlimited sessions, free, with no signup.

How do the notifications work?

When you press Start, the page asks permission to show browser notifications, then alerts you each time a focus or break phase ends.

Where are my stats stored?

In your browser's local storage under this site. The daily count resets each day and never leaves your device.

What is the Pomodoro technique?

A time-management method: work in focused 25-minute intervals (pomodoros) with 5-minute breaks, taking a longer break after four rounds.
