eSign PDF

Draw your signature and place it on any PDF.

Click anywhere on the page outline to place your signature there, or use the sliders below. Click "Add signature" to confirm the position — you can add several.

How to use eSign PDF

  1. Upload your PDF file using the file picker.
  2. Draw your signature on the signature pad with your mouse or finger.
  3. Select a page, click on the page outline (or use the sliders) to position the signature, add it, then download the signed PDF.

What is eSign PDF?

eSign PDF is a free browser-based tool that lets you draw a handwritten-style signature and stamp it onto any PDF document. It is ideal for signing contracts, agreements, and forms without printing, scanning, or installing dedicated e-signature software.

How it works

Your signature drawing is captured from the on-screen pad as a transparent PNG. The PDF is opened with the pdf-lib library directly in your browser, and your signature image is embedded at the exact coordinates you choose on each selected page. You can place multiple signatures on different pages before exporting the final document.

Privacy

All processing happens locally on your device. Your PDF and signature never leave your browser — nothing is uploaded to any server.

FAQ

Is eSign PDF free?

Yes. It is completely free with no limits and no hidden costs.

Do I need an account?

No. No signup or account is required — everything runs directly in your browser.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDF is processed entirely on your device with JavaScript. No file ever leaves your browser.

What is eSign PDF best used for?

It is best for quickly signing contracts, forms, and documents when you need a fast electronic signature without installing software.
