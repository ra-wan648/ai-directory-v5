Image Splitter

Slice an image into an even grid of tiles — great for Instagram carousels, puzzles, and sprite sheets. Tiles download as PNGs, named by row and column.

How to use

  1. Choose a PNG, JPG, or WebP image — it is decoded locally and never uploaded.
  2. Set the grid from 1 to 10 rows and 1 to 10 columns, then press Split image.
  3. Preview every tile below and download each as a PNG, or use Download all tiles to grab the whole set at once.

How tiles are cut

The image is divided into equal tiles of (width ÷ columns) × (height ÷ rows) pixels. Tiles are named tile-r1-c1.png, tile-r1-c2.png, and so on — row first, then column. Any leftover edge pixels are trimmed from the right and bottom edges.

Private by design

Splitting uses the Canvas API entirely in your browser. Your image never touches a server, so there are no upload limits or privacy worries.

Frequently asked questions

What image formats can I split?

Any image your browser can decode — PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and AVIF in most browsers. Tiles are always exported as PNG.

How are tile sizes calculated?

The image is divided into equal tiles using floor division — each tile is (width ÷ columns) × (height ÷ rows) pixels. Any leftover edge pixels are cropped from the right and bottom.

Is my image uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is decoded and sliced with the Canvas API entirely in your browser.

Why does "Download all" need multiple clicks?

Browsers download tiles one file at a time and may ask permission for multiple downloads — click Allow in the browser prompt to finish the batch.
