How to use GPA Calculator

  1. Add your courses — For each course enter the name, credit hours, grade (A+ through F) and the semester it belongs to.
  2. Read your GPA — The calculator shows your cumulative weighted GPA plus a per-semester breakdown — all updated live.
  3. Try what-if — Enter a target GPA, your completed credits and planned credits to see the average grade you need going forward.

What is GPA Calculator?

The GPA Calculator computes your weighted grade point average on the standard 4.0 scale, including +/- grades, grouped by semester so you can see trends across terms.

How this education tool works

Each course contributes grade points × credits to the total; the sum divided by total credits is your GPA. The what-if planner rearranges that same equation to solve for the average grade you need in your remaining credits to reach a target GPA.

Privacy and limits

All course data stays in the page's memory — nothing is uploaded or saved. The 4.0 scale used here matches the common US convention; check your institution if yours differs (some use 4.3 or percentage scales).

GPA Calculator — frequently asked questions

What grade scale does this GPA calculator use?

The standard US 4.0 scale: A+/A = 4.0, A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B- = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, C- = 1.7, D+ = 1.3, D = 1.0, D- = 0.7, F = 0.0.

How is weighted GPA calculated?

Each course's grade points are multiplied by its credit hours, the results are summed, and the sum is divided by total credit hours. A 4-credit A counts twice as much as a 2-credit A.

How does the what-if target calculator work?

Enter your current cumulative GPA and completed credits, a target GPA, and how many credits you still plan to take. It solves for the average grade points you need in the remaining credits — and tells you if the target is mathematically impossible.

Is my course data saved anywhere?

No. Everything lives in the page's memory and vanishes when you close the tab. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
