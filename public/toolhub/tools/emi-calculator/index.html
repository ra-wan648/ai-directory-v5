What is Loan EMI Calculator?

The Loan EMI Calculator computes your monthly instalment with the standard reducing-balance formula, then builds a complete amortization schedule showing how each payment splits into principal and interest.

How this utilitie tool works

Everything is derived in one pass: monthly rate = annual ÷ 12 ÷ 100, factor = (1 + r)^n, and each period's interest is charged on the remaining balance. The year view aggregates twelve months at a time.

Privacy and limits

All processing stays in your browser and figures are for planning only — they do not constitute financial advice. Very long tenures (hundreds of months) render fine but the table is long.

Loan EMI Calculator — frequently asked questions

What formula does the EMI calculator use?

The standard reducing-balance formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r the monthly interest rate and n the number of months. A zero interest rate falls back to P / n.

Can I see a year-by-year breakdown?

Yes. Switch between the per-month and per-year amortization views. Each shows EMI paid, principal repaid, interest paid and the remaining balance.

What does the principal-vs-interest bar show?

The share of your total repayment that goes to principal versus interest — a quick visual of the true cost of the loan.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is an estimate for planning. Actual loan terms, fees and rounding may differ from your lender's schedule.
