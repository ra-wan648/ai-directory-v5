How to use PDF Rotator

  1. Upload your PDF file using the file picker.
  2. Pick a rotation angle (90°, 180° or 270°) and choose all pages or a custom page range.
  3. Click "Rotate and download" to apply the rotation and save the new PDF.

What is PDF Rotator?

PDF Rotator is a free tool that rotates pages of a PDF document clockwise by 90, 180, or 270 degrees. It fixes scanned pages saved sideways and lets you rotate only specific pages while leaving the rest untouched.

How it works

Your PDF is opened in the browser with the pdf-lib library. The chosen angle is added to each selected page's existing rotation (normalized to 0–360°), then the document is saved and downloaded as a new file. Your original file is never modified.

Privacy

All processing happens locally on your device. Your PDF never leaves your browser and nothing is uploaded to any server.

FAQ

Is PDF Rotator free?

Yes. It is completely free with no limits and no hidden costs.

Do I need an account?

No. No signup or account is required — everything runs directly in your browser.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDF is processed entirely on your device with JavaScript. No file ever leaves your browser.

What is PDF Rotator best used for?

It is best for fixing scanned documents or photos saved sideways, and for rotating individual pages of a PDF without affecting the rest.
