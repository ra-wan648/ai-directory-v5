What is AI Background Remover?

AI Background Remover separates the subject of a photo from its background using a real segmentation model (RMBG-1.4) — no green screen, no manual tracing. Perfect for product photos, profile pictures, thumbnails and design cut-outs.

How this image tool works

When you click Remove Background, the page lazily loads the Transformers.js engine and the RMBG-1.4 model (downloaded once, then cached). The model predicts a foreground mask, which is composited over your photo so the background becomes fully transparent — all on your own device.

Privacy and limits

Everything runs 100% locally — no photo is ever uploaded. Best results come from photos with a clear subject; very busy or low-contrast backgrounds may need a second look. Large photos are processed at full resolution, which can take a few seconds on slower devices.

AI Background Remover — frequently asked questions

Is the AI background remover free?

Yes. The AI Background Remover on AI Directory is completely free — no account, no watermark and no limit on how many images you process.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The AI model runs 100% on your device with Transformers.js. Your images never leave your browser, so the tool is safe for private photos.

Why does the first run take longer?

The first removal downloads the AI model (~40MB). Your browser caches it, so every run after that starts instantly with no download.

Which formats can I upload, and what do I get back?

Upload PNG, JPG or WebP. You can download the result as a transparent PNG, ready to use in designs, thumbnails and product photos.
