What is the vCard QR Generator?

The vCard QR Generator turns your contact details into a QR code that phones understand as a digital business card. When someone scans it, their phone offers to save you as a contact — no typing, no misspelled email addresses. It encodes the standard vCard 3.0 format, which is supported by iPhone, Android and most contact apps.

How this social & business tool works

Everything happens in your browser: your details are converted into vCard text and drawn as a QR code on the page itself. Nothing is uploaded or stored, which is exactly what you want for personal contact data. The Download button grabs the rendered QR image straight from the page and saves it as a PNG, ready for print.

vCard QR Generator — frequently asked questions

Is the vCard QR Generator free to use?

Yes. It is completely free with no signup, no watermarks and no limits on how many codes you generate.

Will the QR code work with my phone's camera?

On modern iPhones and Android phones, yes — scanning the code opens an “Add contact” prompt because it uses the standard vCard 3.0 format.

Is my contact information sent to a server?

No. The vCard text and the QR code are generated entirely in your browser, so your details never leave your device.

What should I do if a printed code won't scan?

Keep the details concise, print it at least 2×2 cm, and use high contrast (dark code on a light background). Very long addresses can make the code dense and harder to scan.
