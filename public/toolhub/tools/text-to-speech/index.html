Text to Speech

Convert text to natural speech with free browser voices.

How to use Text to Speech

  1. Type or paste the text you want spoken aloud.
  2. Choose a voice from the dropdown and adjust the rate and pitch sliders.
  3. Click "Speak". Use Pause, Resume, or Stop to control playback.

What is Text to Speech?

Text to Speech is a free tool that reads your text aloud using the voices built into your browser and operating system. It is great for listening to articles, proofreading your writing by hearing it read back, and accessibility.

How it works

The tool uses the Web Speech API (speechSynthesis) available in modern browsers. The available voices depend on your device — most systems include several languages and accents at no cost. Rate and pitch are applied to every utterance in real time.

Privacy

All speech synthesis happens on your device. Your text is never sent to any server.

FAQ

Is Text to Speech free?

Yes. It is completely free with no limits and no hidden costs.

Do I need an account?

No. No signup or account is required — everything runs directly in your browser.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. Your text is spoken using voices built into your device and browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

What is Text to Speech best used for?

It is best for listening to articles, proofreading your writing by hearing it read aloud, and accessibility for anyone who prefers spoken text.
