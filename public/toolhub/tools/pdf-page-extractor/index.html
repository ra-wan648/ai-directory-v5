PDF Page Extractor

Extract selected pages from a PDF into a new file.

How to use PDF Page Extractor

  1. Upload your PDF file using the file picker.
  2. Tick the pages you want, or type a range like 1-3,5.
  3. Click "Extract selected pages" to build a new PDF and download it.

What is PDF Page Extractor?

PDF Page Extractor is a free tool that pulls chosen pages out of a PDF and saves them as a brand-new document. It is perfect for splitting reports, saving a single chapter, or removing pages you no longer need.

How it works

The PDF is opened in your browser with the pdf-lib library. The pages you select are copied — in page-number order — into a fresh PDF document, which you can then download. Page ranges like 2-4,7 are parsed and validated against the document's page count.

Privacy

All processing happens locally on your device. Your PDF never leaves your browser and nothing is uploaded to any server.

FAQ

Is PDF Page Extractor free?

Yes. It is completely free with no limits and no hidden costs.

Do I need an account?

No. No signup or account is required — everything runs directly in your browser.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDF is processed entirely on your device with JavaScript. No file ever leaves your browser.

What is PDF Page Extractor best used for?

It is best for splitting a large PDF into smaller documents, pulling out a single chapter, or removing pages you do not need.
