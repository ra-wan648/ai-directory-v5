    How to use Voice Recorder

    1. Click "Record" and allow microphone access when your browser asks.
    2. Speak while watching the live timer and level meter.
    3. Click "Stop", then play the recording in the list or download it as a .webm file.

    What is Voice Recorder?

    Voice Recorder is a free browser-based tool for capturing audio with your device's microphone. It is handy for voice notes, interview clips, narration takes, and any quick audio you want to record and keep as a downloadable file.

    How it works

    The tool uses the browser's MediaRecorder API to capture microphone audio in the WebM format, with an AnalyserNode powering the live level meter. When you stop, the recording is assembled into a file you can play back immediately or download.

    Privacy

    All recording and processing happens locally on your device. Your audio never leaves your browser and nothing is uploaded to any server.

    FAQ

    Is Voice Recorder free?

    Yes. It is completely free with no limits and no hidden costs.

    Do I need an account?

    No. No signup or account is required — everything runs directly in your browser.

    Is my recording uploaded to a server?

    No. Your recordings stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

    What is Voice Recorder best used for?

    It is best for quick voice notes, interview clips, podcast test takes, and any audio you want to capture fast and download as a file.
