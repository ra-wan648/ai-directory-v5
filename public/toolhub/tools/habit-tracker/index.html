Habit Tracker

Track daily habits and build streaks.

How to use

  1. Add a habit — Name it, pick a color, and press Add. Your habits are saved in this browser.
  2. Tap days to check off — Tap a day cell to mark the habit done. Tap again to undo. Today is outlined in amber.
  3. Watch streaks grow — Each habit shows its current streak — consecutive days completed — plus your all-time total.

What is the Habit Tracker?

The Habit Tracker helps you build consistency with a simple 7-day grid per habit. Add habits like “read 20 pages” or “no sugar”, tap each day you follow through, and watch your streak counter climb. Streaks count consecutive completed days, so missing a day resets the count — the honest feedback loop that makes habits stick.

How this education tool works

Habits and their checkmarks are stored in your browser's local storage, so your tracker persists across visits on this device with no account and no server. The grid always shows the last 7 days ending today, and the streak is computed from your actual check history each time the page loads.

Habit Tracker — frequently asked questions

Is the Habit Tracker free to use?

Yes. Track unlimited habits, free, with no signup.

Where is my habit data stored?

In your browser's local storage on this device. It persists across visits but isn't synced anywhere else.

How is the streak calculated?

Consecutive days with the habit checked, counting back from today (or yesterday if today isn't checked yet). One missed day resets it.

Can I delete a habit?

Yes — the × button on each habit removes it and its history after a confirmation.
