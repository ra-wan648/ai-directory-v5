Meeting Cost Calculator — frequently asked questions

How is meeting cost calculated?

Cost per meeting = attendees × average hourly rate × duration in hours. Weekly burn multiplies that by meetings per week; monthly uses 4.33 weeks and yearly uses 52 weeks.

What hourly rate should I use?

Use the average fully-loaded hourly cost of the attendees — salary plus benefits and overhead — not just take-home pay. A common rough estimate is 1.3× the average hourly salary.

How does the live meeting ticker work?

When you press “Start meeting”, the page tracks real elapsed seconds and multiplies by the per-second burn rate (attendees × hourly rate ÷ 3600), updating the ticker every second. Changing inputs pauses it for accuracy.

Is any of my data saved?

No. All calculations happen in your browser and nothing is stored or uploaded. Close the tab and everything is gone.
