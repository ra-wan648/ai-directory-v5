What is Savings Goal Calculator?

The Savings Goal Calculator turns a savings target into a concrete monthly plan — or tells you how long a fixed monthly deposit will take to reach the goal, with interest doing part of the work.

How this finance tool works

It uses the future-value-of-annuity formula with monthly compounding, solving for the payment or for the number of periods depending on the mode. The bar chart splits the final balance into your deposits versus earned interest.

Privacy and limits

All math runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored. Results are estimates for planning, not financial advice.

Savings Goal Calculator — frequently asked questions

How is the required monthly deposit calculated?

With the future-value-of-annuity formula, assuming deposits at the end of each month and monthly compounding: PMT = (Goal - Current x (1+r)^n) x r / ((1+r)^n - 1). For example, to reach $12,000 in one year at 6% starting from zero, you need about $972.80 per month.

What does the reverse mode do?

Reverse mode answers the opposite question: given a fixed monthly deposit, how many months until you reach the goal? It solves the same compound-interest equation for time instead of payment.

Is the interest rate nominal or effective?

Enter the nominal annual rate; the tool compounds it monthly (rate / 12 per month), which matches how most savings accounts accrue interest.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. All calculations run in JavaScript in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.
