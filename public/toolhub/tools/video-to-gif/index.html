How to use

  1. Pick a video file — choose an MP4, WebM or MOV file from your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. Set the clip options — enter the start time and duration in seconds, then choose a frame rate (10 or 15 fps) and width (320 or 480 px).
  3. Convert and download — click Convert to GIF. The engine builds a palette-optimized GIF and your download starts automatically.

Why this GIF converter uses two passes

GIFs can only hold 256 colors, so picking the right palette decides the quality. This tool first analyzes your exact clip with palettegen, then renders the GIF with paletteuse against that custom palette — producing noticeably cleaner colors and less banding than a single-pass conversion.

Tips for small, high-quality GIFs

GIF file size grows with duration, frame rate and width. For a GIF that loads fast anywhere, keep clips under 5 seconds at 10 fps and 320 px. Use 15 fps and 480 px when smoothness and sharpness matter more than file size.

Frequently asked questions

Is the video to GIF converter free?

Yes. The Video to GIF Converter on AI Directory is completely free — no account, no watermark, and no limit on how many clips you convert.

Does my video get uploaded to a server?

No. The FFmpeg engine runs entirely in your browser via WebAssembly. Your video file never leaves your device.

Why does the conversion use two passes?

The tool first generates a color palette from your exact clip (palettegen), then renders the GIF using that palette (paletteuse). This two-pass method produces noticeably better colors and less banding than a single-pass conversion.

How long can the GIF be?

The duration is capped at 30 seconds. GIFs get very large quickly, so for small file sizes use a short duration, 10 fps and 320 px width.
