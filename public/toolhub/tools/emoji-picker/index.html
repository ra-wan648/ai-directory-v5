Emoji Picker

Search and copy emojis fast. Free, no signup, and everything runs in your browser.

How to use Emoji Picker

  1. Search or browse — Type a word like heart or fire in the search box, or tap a category tab to browse.
  2. Click an emoji — Click any emoji to copy it to your clipboard instantly.
  3. Paste anywhere — Paste it into your message, document, caption or post. Frequently used emojis appear in the Recent row.

What is the Emoji Picker?

A fast, searchable emoji gallery with over 150 curated emojis across eight categories: smileys, people, nature, food, activity, travel, objects and symbols. Search by name or browse by tab, then click to copy.

How does copying work?

Clicking an emoji copies it to your clipboard using the browser's copy API, with an automatic fallback for older browsers. A small toast confirms each copy so you always know it worked.

What is the Recent row?

Emojis you copy are saved to a Recent row stored in your browser's local storage — the most useful ones stay one click away the next time you visit, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Emoji Picker — frequently asked questions

Is the Emoji Picker free?

Yes — free with no signup and no limits.

How many emojis are included?

Over 150 curated emojis across 8 categories, each searchable by name.

Does it remember my recent emojis?

Yes. Your recently copied emojis are saved in your browser's local storage and shown in a Recent row on your next visit.

Do emojis work on every platform?

Emojis are standard Unicode characters, so they render on phones, computers and social apps. The exact artwork varies by device and operating system.
