How to use Salary Calculator

  1. Choose your starting point — Pick whether you know your gross or your net (take-home) salary.
  2. Enter amount, frequency and tax — Type the salary amount, choose monthly or yearly pay, then add your tax rate and any monthly deductions.
  3. Read the breakdown — The table shows gross, tax, deductions and net for both monthly and yearly pay.

What is Salary Calculator?

The Salary Calculator converts between gross and net salary in either direction: enter what you know, and it works out the rest using a simple flat-tax model with your own deduction figure.

How this utilitie tool works

It recomputes instantly on every keystroke: net = gross × (1 − tax) − deductions, or the inverse gross = (net + deductions) / (1 − tax). Monthly and yearly columns are kept in sync.

Privacy and limits

The result is an estimate for planning and negotiation — actual take-home pay depends on tax brackets, allowances and benefits. Your numbers never leave this page.

Salary Calculator — frequently asked questions

How does the calculator convert net salary back to gross?

It reverses the flat-tax math: gross = (net + deductions) / (1 − tax rate). For example, a 30,000 net with 10% tax and no deductions means a gross of 33,333.33.

Are both monthly and yearly figures shown?

Yes. The breakdown table shows gross, tax, deductions and net side by side for both monthly and yearly pay.

Does it handle tax brackets or employer contributions?

No — it uses one flat tax rate plus a flat monthly deduction you enter. It is a quick estimator, not a payroll engine or tax advice.

Is my salary data private?

Yes. All calculations happen in your browser; nothing is sent to any server or stored.
