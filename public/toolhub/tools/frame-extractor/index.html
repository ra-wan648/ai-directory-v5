Video Frame Extractor

Capture still frames from any video as JPG images at exact timestamps or fixed intervals. No heavy engine download — frames are captured directly from the HTML5 video player into a canvas, all on your device.

Your file never leaves your device.

How to use

  1. Pick a video file — choose an MP4, WebM or MOV file from your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. Choose timestamps or an interval — type a comma-separated list of timestamps in seconds (e.g. 1.5, 4, 10.25), or switch to interval mode and enter how many seconds apart each frame should be.
  3. Extract and download — click Extract frames. Each frame is captured as a full-resolution JPG with a thumbnail preview and its own download link.

How frame extraction works

Your video is loaded into a hidden HTML5 <video> player. For each timestamp the player seeks to that exact moment, the current picture is painted onto a <canvas>, and the canvas is exported as a JPG. No conversion engine is downloaded, so extraction starts instantly.

Choosing timestamps

Timestamps are in seconds from the start of the video and can be fractional — 0.5 grabs half a second in. Interval mode walks through the whole video from 0 seconds, which is ideal for storyboards or finding a specific moment. Up to 100 frames per run.

Frequently asked questions

Is the video frame extractor free?

Yes. The Video Frame Extractor on AI Directory is completely free — no account, no watermark, and no limit on how many frames you capture.

Does my video get uploaded to a server?

No. Frame extraction uses the HTML5 video element and canvas in your own browser. Your video file never leaves your device.

What image format do the frames come in?

Frames are exported as JPG images at 92% quality, at the video's own resolution (capped at 1280 px wide for very large sources).

How many frames can I extract at once?

Up to 100 frames per run. For more, run the extraction again with a different timestamp list or interval.
