How to use Business Name Generator

  1. Enter a keyword — Type a word that describes your business — for example “pixel”, “bloom” or “forge” — and pick your industry.
  2. Choose a style — Pick modern for startup-style mashups, playful for fun memorable names, or professional for trustworthy two-word names.
  3. Generate and copy — Hit Generate names to get 30 ideas. Click any name to copy it to your clipboard instantly.

What is Business Name Generator?

The Business Name Generator turns one keyword into 30 brandable name ideas. It blends your keyword with curated prefix and suffix lists across 8 industries and 3 styles — mashups like “Pixelhub”, pairs like “Nova Forge”, and polished two-word names like “Forge & Co.”.

How this social & business tool works

Names are built locally in your browser by combining your keyword with 40+ prefixes (Nova, Aero, Lumen…), 40+ suffixes (hub, ify, labs…) and industry flavor words, then shuffling the results so every generation feels fresh. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Privacy and limits

Your keyword and results stay on your device. Generated names are creative starting points — always check trademarks, domains and social handles before using one for a real business.

Business Name Generator — frequently asked questions

How does the business name generator create names?

It combines your keyword with curated prefix and suffix word lists (40+ each) using proven naming patterns — keyword+suffix mashups, prefix+keyword pairs, and two-word professional names — then shuffles them so every generation is unique.

Can I use a generated name as my legal business name?

The ideas are a starting point, not legal advice. Before committing, check that the name is not already trademarked and that the domain and social handles are available in your country.

Is the business name generator free?

Yes — completely free, unlimited generations, no account and no signup. Everything runs in your browser.

Is my keyword stored or sent anywhere?

No. Your keyword never leaves your device — names are generated locally with JavaScript and nothing is saved or uploaded.
