Camera Photo Capture

Snap a photo with your device's camera right in the browser — no app needed. Capture the frame, try a filter, and download it as a PNG.

Resolution: —
Press Start camera to begin. Camera access requires HTTPS (or localhost).
Your video stream and photos never leave this browser — nothing is recorded or uploaded.

How to use

  1. Press Start camera and allow camera access when your browser asks.
  2. When the live preview looks right, press Capture to freeze the frame.
  3. Pick a filter — none, grayscale, sepia, or invert — then press Download PNG to save the photo.

Permission denied?

If the camera won't start, click the camera icon in your browser's address bar and allow access for this site, then press Start camera again. Also make sure no other app (Zoom, Teams, another tab) is holding the camera.

Private by design

The video stream, the captured frame, and filter processing all happen locally. Nothing is uploaded, recorded, or sent anywhere — the photo only leaves your device when you choose to download it.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the camera not starting?

Camera access requires HTTPS (or localhost) and your permission. If access was denied, click the camera icon in the address bar to allow it, then press Start camera again. Also check no other app is using the camera.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The video stream, the captured frame, and the filter processing all stay in your browser. The photo only leaves your device when you download it.

Can I change the filter after capturing?

Yes. Changing the filter re-renders the captured frame instantly, so you can compare looks before downloading.

What resolution is the photo?

The photo matches your camera's stream resolution as delivered by the browser — typically 640×480 to 1920×1080 depending on the device.
