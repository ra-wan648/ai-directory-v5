What is Text Summarizer?

The Text Summarizer condenses long text into its most important sentences. It is extractive, meaning it selects real sentences from your text rather than rewriting them, so quotes and facts stay exactly as written.

How this writing tool works

Your text is split into sentences and every word is counted. Common stopwords (the, and, of, and about 90 more) are ignored, each sentence is scored by the frequency of its meaningful words, and the highest-scoring sentences are returned in their original order — all with plain JavaScript in your browser.

Privacy and limits

All processing happens locally in the page. Nothing is uploaded or stored, and the tool works offline. Very long texts (tens of thousands of words) may take a moment to score.

Text Summarizer — frequently asked questions

Is this an AI summarizer?

No. This is an extractive summarizer: it scores each sentence by word frequency (ignoring common stopwords) and keeps the highest-scoring sentences in their original order. It runs entirely in your browser with plain JavaScript, so it works offline and never sends your text anywhere.

How does the summarizer pick sentences?

The text is split into sentences, each word is counted, and common stopwords like "the" and "and" are ignored. Every sentence gets a score from the frequencies of its meaningful words, and the top-scoring sentences are returned in their original order.

What do the compression stats mean?

Compression is the summary's word count as a percentage of the original. For example, 40% means the summary keeps 40% of the original words. The panel also shows sentence and word counts for both versions.

Does my text leave my browser?

No. All scoring and summarizing happens locally in the page. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent to any server.
