Why meta tags matter

  
Search engines use your title and description to build search result snippets, while Open Graph and Twitter card tags control how your page looks when shared on social networks — the headline, summary, and preview image in link cards.

  
Only what you fill in

  
The generator only outputs tags for fields you actually fill in, so your <head> stays clean with no empty or placeholder tags.






  
Frequently asked questions

  
What meta tags does this tool generate?
Standard SEO tags (title, description, keywords, author, canonical, robots, theme-color) plus Open Graph tags for Facebook/LinkedIn and Twitter card tags for rich social previews.

  
Where do I paste the generated tags?
Paste them inside the <head> section of your HTML page, ideally before any render-blocking scripts.

  
Why do I need Open Graph tags?
Open Graph tags control how your page looks when shared on social networks — the title, description, and image shown in link previews.

  
Is this Meta Tag Generator free?
Yes, it is completely free, runs entirely in your browser, and requires no signup.
