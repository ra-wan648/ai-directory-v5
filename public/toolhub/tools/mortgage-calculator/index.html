What is Mortgage Calculator?

The Mortgage Calculator estimates your monthly principal-and-interest payment for a fixed-rate home loan, shows how much interest you will pay over the life of the loan, and breaks the payoff down year by year.

How this finance tool works

It uses the standard amortization formula M = P × r(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), then simulates every monthly payment to build the amortization schedule. Extra payments are applied straight to principal in the simulation.

Privacy and limits

All math runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored. This is an estimate for planning, not a loan offer; taxes, insurance and fees are excluded.

Mortgage Calculator — frequently asked questions

How is the monthly payment calculated?

With the standard amortization formula: M = P x r(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P is the loan amount, r the monthly interest rate and n the number of payments. For example, $300,000 at 6% over 30 years gives about $1,798.65 per month in principal and interest.

Does this include taxes and insurance?

No. The calculator shows principal and interest (P&I) only. Property tax, homeowner's insurance and PMI/HOA are not included, so your real monthly housing cost will be higher.

How do extra payments help?

Extra monthly payments go straight to principal, which shrinks the balance faster and cuts the total interest. Even $100 extra per month on a 30-year loan can save tens of thousands in interest and shave years off the term.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. All calculations run in JavaScript in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.
