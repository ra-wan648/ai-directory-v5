How to use IBAN Validator


  1. Paste the IBAN — Enter or paste the IBAN with or without spaces — spacing and lowercase are normalized automatically.
  2. Validate — Click Validate IBAN. Each rule is checked in order: format, country code, length, then the mod-97 checksum.
  3. Read the breakdown — A valid IBAN shows its country, check digits, BBAN and a pretty groups-of-four print. An invalid one tells you exactly which rule failed.




What is IBAN Validator?
IBAN Validator checks International Bank Account Numbers the way banks do — structural rules plus the ISO 13616 mod-97 checksum — and explains failures instead of just saying “invalid”.
How this developer tool works
Input is normalized, then checked step by step: character set, known country code, country-specific length, and finally the mod-97 remainder computed digit-by-digit to avoid any precision loss.
Privacy and limits
Everything runs locally. Validation confirms the IBAN is well-formed — it cannot confirm the account exists or belongs to anyone.




IBAN Validator — frequently asked questions


How does IBAN validation work?
The first four characters move to the end, letters convert to numbers (A=10 … Z=35), and the resulting integer must leave remainder 1 when divided by 97. This mod-97 check catches virtually all typos and transpositions.
Which countries are supported?
About 50 IBAN countries including all of the EU/EEA, the UK, Switzerland, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Turkey, Israel, Brazil and more — each with its official IBAN length.
What are the “check digits”?
Digits 3–4 of the IBAN. They are computed from the rest of the number with the mod-97 algorithm, so any single error in the IBAN almost always breaks the check.
Is my IBAN sent to a server?
No. Validation is pure arithmetic done locally in your browser. That said, treat real account numbers as sensitive and avoid pasting them into any website you do not trust.
