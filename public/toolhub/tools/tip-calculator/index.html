How to use

  1. Enter the bill amount (pre-tip total).
  2. Drag the tip percentage slider from 0% to 50%.
  3. Set the number of people with the stepper — the tip, total, and per-person shares update instantly.

The math

Tip = bill × (tip % ÷ 100). Total = bill + tip. Per-person share = total ÷ people. All figures are rounded to the nearest cent.

Pre-tax convention

Tip is customarily calculated on the pre-tax subtotal, not the final total. Enter the pre-tax amount in the bill field to follow that convention.

Frequently asked questions

How is the tip calculated?

Tip = bill × (tip % ÷ 100). The total is bill + tip, and the per-person share is total ÷ number of people.

What is a standard tip percentage?

In the US, 15–20% is standard for sit-down restaurants and 18–22% for great service. In many other countries 10% is common — when in doubt, follow local custom.

Is the tip calculated before or after tax?

Customarily the tip is calculated on the pre-tax subtotal. This calculator tips on whatever amount you enter, so enter the pre-tax total to follow the standard convention.
