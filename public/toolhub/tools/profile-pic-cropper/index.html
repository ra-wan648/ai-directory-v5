Profile Picture Cropper

Crop and frame the perfect circular profile picture.

Tip: drag the photo to reposition it. You can also scroll over the image to zoom.

How to use

  1. Upload a photo — Choose any JPG or PNG from your device. It loads into the editor instantly — nothing is uploaded.
  2. Drag and zoom inside the circle — Drag the photo to reposition it and use the slider (or your mouse wheel) to zoom until the framing is right.
  3. Download the 512px PNG — Export a crisp square PNG with your circular crop on a transparent background, ready for any profile.

What is the Profile Picture Cropper?

The Profile Picture Cropper frames the perfect circular avatar for LinkedIn, Twitter, WhatsApp, Discord and anywhere else that crops your photo into a circle. Upload a picture, drag it into position, zoom to taste, and download a 512×512 PNG with transparency outside the circle — so it looks right on light and dark backgrounds alike.

How this social & business tool works

Your photo is drawn onto a canvas in your browser and clipped to a circle; the export canvas is exactly 512 pixels square with a transparent surround. Because the whole process is local, even large camera photos never leave your device, and the download is generated straight from the canvas.

Profile Picture Cropper — frequently asked questions

Is the Profile Picture Cropper free to use?

Yes. Crop and download as many profile pictures as you want, free, with no signup.

What size is the exported image?

A 512×512 pixel PNG — sharp enough for every major social network's avatar slot.

Is the area outside the circle transparent?

Yes. The download is a square PNG with transparency outside the circle, so it blends into any background.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The image is loaded and cropped entirely in your browser and is discarded when you leave the page.
