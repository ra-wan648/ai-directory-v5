How to use

  1. Pick a duration — Choose 15, 30 or 60 seconds, then press Start. The clock begins immediately, so be ready.
  2. Type the words shown — Type each word and press space to move on. Correct words turn green, mistakes turn red as you go.
  3. See your score — When time runs out you get WPM, accuracy and consistency — then hit Restart to try to beat it.

What is the Typing Speed Test?

The Typing Speed Test measures how fast and accurately you type using a stream of common English words. Choose 15, 30 or 60 seconds, type what you see, and get three numbers at the end: WPM (words per minute), accuracy, and consistency — the last one showing whether you type at a steady pace or in bursts.

How this education tool works

The test is written from scratch in plain JavaScript (not copied from any existing typing app). Every keystroke is timed in your browser to compute live WPM and per-second consistency; WPM counts five correct characters as one word, the standard definition. Nothing is uploaded or stored — your results exist only until you close the tab.

Typing Speed Test — frequently asked questions

Is the Typing Speed Test free to use?

Yes. Run unlimited tests, free, with no signup and no saved scores.

How is WPM calculated?

Standard net WPM: correct characters typed divided by five, divided by minutes elapsed. Only correctly typed characters count.

What does consistency mean?

How steady your speed is second by second. 100% means a perfectly even pace; big bursts and pauses lower it.

Are my scores saved anywhere?

No. The test runs fully in your browser and scores disappear when you leave the page.
