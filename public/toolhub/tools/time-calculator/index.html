What is Time Calculator?

The Time Calculator does the clock arithmetic spreadsheets make awkward: adding up work sessions, subtracting breaks, measuring gaps between timestamps, and converting the decimal hours timesheets love into readable hh:mm.

How this math tool works

A small parser reads durations like 2h30m, 90m or 1:30 into seconds, evaluates the +/− expression left to right, and renders the total back into days, hours, minutes and decimal form — all in JavaScript on this page.

Privacy and limits

Time Calculator — frequently asked questions

Which duration formats are understood?

The parser accepts 2h30m, 2h 30m, 90m, 1.5h, 45s and clock-style 1:30 (1 hour 30 minutes) or 1:30:00. Units can be combined freely, e.g. 1h 20m 30s.

How do I write an expression?

Join durations with + and -: for example '2h30m + 1h45m - 30m'. A leading minus makes a duration negative. Whitespace is ignored.

How does the time-difference section work?

Pick two date-times. The tool shows the signed difference (end minus start) broken into days, hours, minutes and seconds, plus the total in each unit.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. All parsing and math run in JavaScript in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.
