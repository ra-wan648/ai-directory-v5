How to use

  1. Press Flip coin — the 3D coin spins and lands on heads or tails.
  2. Every result is added to your running heads/tails tally, saved on this device.
  3. Optionally pick Best of 3, 5, or 7 to play a full match — the first side to take the majority wins.

How the 3D flip works

The coin is two circular faces in a 3D scene: heads on the front, tails rotated 180° on the back. Each flip adds several full spins plus the half-turn needed to land on the result, eased with a smooth deceleration so it feels like a real toss settling.

Fairness you can trust

The result is a single random bit from crypto.getRandomValues() — exactly 50/50, with no animation trickery deciding the outcome. The flip is chosen first; the animation merely reveals it.

Frequently asked questions

Is the coin flip fair?

Yes. Each result is a single random bit from crypto.getRandomValues, giving exactly 50/50 odds with no predictable pattern.

What is best-of-N mode?

Best-of-N turns flips into a match: the first side to win the majority of N flips (2 of 3, 3 of 5, or 4 of 7) takes the match, and a winner banner appears.

Is my tally saved?

Yes, your lifetime heads/tails tally is stored in your browser's localStorage on this device. You can reset it any time with the Reset tally button.

Is this Coin Flip free?

Yes, it is completely free, runs entirely in your browser, and requires no signup.
