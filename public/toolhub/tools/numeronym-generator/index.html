How to use

  1. Type one or more words, separated by spaces or line breaks.
  2. Pick how many leading and trailing letters to keep (1–3 each). The classic i18n style keeps 1 and 1.
  3. Each word is shown next to its numeronym — copy a single pair or the whole list.

What is a numeronym?

A numeronym compresses a word to its first N letters, the number of letters removed from the middle, and its last N letters. Tech classics include i18n (internationalization), a11y (accessibility), l10n (localization), and k8s (kubernetes).

Character counting

Length is counted in Unicode code points, so emoji and accented characters each count as one letter. A word must be longer than the kept letters plus at least one middle letter — shorter words are left unchanged.

Frequently asked questions

What is a numeronym?

A numeronym is a word shortened to its first N letters, the count of the removed middle letters, and its last N letters. Examples: internationalization becomes i18n, accessibility becomes a11y, and localization becomes l10n.

What does "too short — unchanged" mean?

A numeronym only makes sense when the word is longer than the kept letters plus at least one middle letter. If the word is too short, the tool leaves it unchanged instead of producing a misleading result.

Can I process several words at once?

Yes. Enter any number of words separated by spaces or line breaks; each one is converted independently and shown as an original → numeronym pair.
