Acronym Generator

Turn any phrase into acronym variants — first letters, lowercase, vowel-removed, and consonant-only. Great for naming projects, startups, and teams.

First letters (uppercase)
—
Classic acronym: first letter of every word, uppercased.
First letters (lowercase)
—
Handy for usernames, slugs, and package names.
Vowel-removed
—
The whole phrase with a, e, i, o, u stripped out (brand-style short names).
Consonants only
—
Only consonant letters, joined with no spaces.

How to use

  1. Type or paste your phrase, project name, or sentence in the input box.
  2. All four variants update instantly as you type — no button needed.
  3. Hit Copy on the variant you like to copy it to your clipboard.

How the variants are built

Words are split on whitespace. The first-letters variants take the first letter found in each word (number-only segments are skipped). Vowel-removed strips a, e, i, o, u from the entire phrase, and consonant-only keeps just consonant letters.

Nothing leaves your browser

All four variants are computed locally with JavaScript. Your phrases are never uploaded, stored, or sent anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a word when building the acronym?

Words are split on whitespace. The first letter found in each word contributes to the acronym; segments made only of numbers or symbols are skipped.

What do the four variants mean?

First-letters takes the first letter of each word in uppercase (like IBM). Lowercase does the same in lowercase. Vowel-removed strips a, e, i, o, u from the whole phrase. Consonant-only keeps only consonant letters, joined together.

Are my phrases saved or sent anywhere?

No. Everything is computed in your browser with JavaScript; nothing leaves your device.

Is this Acronym Generator free?

Yes, it is completely free, runs entirely in your browser, and requires no signup.
