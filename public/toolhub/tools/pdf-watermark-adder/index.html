What is PDF Watermark Adder?

The PDF Watermark Adder embeds a text stamp — "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", a company name, a copyright line — onto every page of a PDF. Unlike image overlays pasted by hand, the watermark is written into the PDF itself with real font embedding.

How this PDF tool works

The pdf-lib library opens your PDF and draws your text on every page: as a full-page diagonal repeat (the classic official-document look), as a single centered stamp, or as a footer line. Opacity, size, rotation and color are applied exactly as you set them.

Privacy and limits

Everything runs in your browser — your PDF is never uploaded or stored anywhere. Password-protected PDFs need the password removed before watermarking, and very large files may take a few seconds to process.

PDF Watermark Adder — frequently asked questions

Is the PDF watermark adder free?

Yes. The PDF Watermark Adder on AI Directory is completely free — no account, no watermark added to your file by us, and no usage limits.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The watermark is embedded with the pdf-lib library directly in your browser. Your document never leaves your device.

What is "diagonal repeat" watermarking?

Diagonal repeat tiles your watermark text across the whole page at a 45° angle, so it appears behind the content everywhere — the classic "CONFIDENTIAL" or "DRAFT" look used on official documents.

Can I make the watermark subtle?

Yes — lower the opacity slider (10–30% works well), pick a light gray color, and use a large font size with the diagonal repeat position for a professional "behind the text" effect.
