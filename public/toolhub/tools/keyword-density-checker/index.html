What is Keyword Density Checker?

This tool measures how often your target keyword appears relative to the total word count, and surfaces the most frequent words and phrases in your content — a quick sanity check against keyword stuffing.

How this SEO tool works

Your text is tokenized into real words (Unicode-aware), the keyword is matched case-insensitively on whole-word boundaries, and n-gram tables are built with stopword filtering. Everything runs in JavaScript on this page.

Privacy and limits

All analysis happens locally — your content never leaves the browser. Density is a rough guide, not a ranking rule; natural writing always beats hitting a number.

Keyword Density Checker — frequently asked questions

What is a good keyword density?

There is no official target. As a rough guide, most SEO practitioners aim for roughly 1-2% for a primary keyword. Above 3% often reads as keyword stuffing, and Google has repeatedly said there is no ideal density — natural, helpful writing matters far more.

How is density calculated here?

The tool tokenizes your text into words, counts exact occurrences of your keyword or phrase (case-insensitive, whole-word matched), and divides by the total word count. Density = occurrences / total words x 100.

What are the 1/2/3-gram tables?

They show the most frequent single words, two-word phrases and three-word phrases in your text (common stopwords are filtered out). They reveal what your page is really 'about' in the eyes of a simple frequency analysis.

Does my text leave my browser?

No. Tokenization, counting and analysis all run in JavaScript on this page — nothing is uploaded or stored.
