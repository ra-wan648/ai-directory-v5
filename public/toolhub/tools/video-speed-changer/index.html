How to use

  1. Pick a video file — choose an MP4, WebM or MOV file from your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. Choose a speed — select anything from 0.25x slow motion to 4x fast-forward from the dropdown.
  3. Apply and download — click Change speed. The engine re-times video and audio together, then your download starts automatically.

How speed change keeps audio in sync

Speeding up a video naively only changes the pictures. This tool re-times the video track with setpts=PTS/S and the audio track with the atempo filter by exactly the same factor, so dialogue and sound effects stay aligned with the picture from start to finish.

Choosing a speed

Use 0.25x or 0.5x for dramatic slow motion and tutorials, 1.5x–2x for lectures and vlogs you want to skim, and 4x for timelapse-style fast-forward. The atempo filter only accepts factors from 0.5 to 2.0, so 0.25x and 4x are built by chaining two filters internally.

Frequently asked questions

Is the video speed changer free?

Yes. The Video Speed Changer on AI Directory is completely free — no account, no watermark, and no limit on how many videos you process.

Does my video get uploaded to a server?

No. The FFmpeg engine runs entirely in your browser via WebAssembly. Your video file never leaves your device.

Will the audio stay in sync with the video?

Yes. The video track is re-timed with setpts while the audio track is re-timed with the atempo filter, so both tracks change by exactly the same factor and stay in sync.

Why is 4x the maximum speed?

The atempo audio filter only supports factors between 0.5 and 2.0, so larger changes are chained (atempo=2.0,atempo=2.0 for 4x). Beyond 4x the chained filters would degrade audio quality, so the tool caps there.
