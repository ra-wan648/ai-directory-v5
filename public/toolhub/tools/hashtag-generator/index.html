Hashtag Generator

Generate hashtag sets for Instagram, TikTok and X. Free, no signup, and everything runs in your browser.

One word works best. Use letters only — spaces and symbols are cleaned automatically.

How to use Hashtag Generator

  1. Enter a keyword — Type the main topic of your post, for example travel, fitness or smallbusiness.
  2. Generate the sets — Hit Generate to build three hashtag sets: niche tags around your keyword, popular high-reach tags, and branded tags.
  3. Copy and paste — Click any hashtag to copy it, or use Copy all to grab a whole set, then paste it into your caption.

What is the Hashtag Generator?

Enter one keyword and the tool builds three ready-to-paste hashtag sets. Niche tags are built from your keyword with popular suffixes like tips, reels and community; Popular tags are broad high-reach tags used across Instagram, TikTok and X; Branded tags turn your keyword into ownable brand-style tags.

Why use three kinds of hashtags?

Niche tags reach people already interested in your topic, popular tags widen discovery, and branded tags build a tag you can own over time. Mixing all three is the standard approach used by social media managers.

How many hashtags should I use?

Instagram allows up to 30 — most creators use 5–15 relevant ones. TikTok works well with 3–5, and X posts do best with 1–3. Relevance beats volume: pick tags that genuinely match the post.
