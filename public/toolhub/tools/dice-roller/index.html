How to use

  1. Pick a die type — d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, or d100 — depending on your game.
  2. Set how many dice to roll (1–10) and an optional plus or minus modifier.
  3. Press Roll dice and watch the animated shuffle settle into your results, with each die and the total shown.

Fair randomness, no loaded dice

Every face is drawn from crypto.getRandomValues() with rejection sampling, so each outcome is equally likely — no modulo bias, no predictable Math.random() sequences. It is as fair as a physical die, and faster.

How modifiers work

The modifier is added to the sum of all dice after the roll, following standard tabletop notation: 2d6+3 means roll two six-sided dice and add 3 to the total. Negative modifiers work the same way.

Frequently asked questions

Are the dice rolls truly random?

Yes. Every face comes from crypto.getRandomValues, the browser's cryptographically secure generator, with rejection sampling so no face is favored — unlike Math.random, which can show subtle bias.

Which dice can I roll?

d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20, and d100, with 1 to 10 dice per roll and an optional plus or minus modifier, covering standard tabletop RPG notation.

Can I use this for tabletop RPG sessions?

Absolutely. The roller is fair, fast, and needs no signup — handy as a backup die at the table or for online play.

Is this Dice Roller free?

Yes, it is completely free, runs entirely in your browser, and requires no signup.
