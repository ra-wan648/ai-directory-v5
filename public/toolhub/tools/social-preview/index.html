How to use Social Media Preview

  1. Enter your page details — Type the title, description and site name exactly as they appear in your meta tags.
  2. Add an image — Paste an image URL or upload one from your device — it renders instantly via a local object URL.
  3. Check all three cards — Review the Facebook, X and LinkedIn mockups side by side and fix anything that looks cropped or cut off.

What is the Social Media Preview tool?

Paste your Open Graph details and see realistic share-card mockups for Facebook, X (Twitter) and LinkedIn. The cards match each platform's current layout: image ratios, title sizes and description truncation.

Why preview before publishing?

Social platforms cache your link preview when a URL is first shared, so a broken image or cut-off title can stick around for days. Checking the card here takes seconds and catches missing og:image, wrong aspect ratios and overlong titles.

Recommended image sizes

Use 1200×630 for og:image (Facebook, LinkedIn and most platforms) and the same or 1200×675 for X's summary_large_image card. Keep key content centered — platforms crop edges on some layouts.

Social Media Preview — frequently asked questions

Is the Social Media Preview tool free?

Yes — free with no signup and no limits.

Is my uploaded image sent anywhere?

No. Uploaded images are previewed with a local object URL in your browser and never uploaded to any server.

Which platforms are covered?

Facebook link shares, X (Twitter) large-image cards and LinkedIn link posts — the three most common share-card formats.

Why does my real share look different?

Platforms cache previews per URL and each has its own image pipeline. Use the platform's own debugger (Facebook Sharing Debugger, X Card Validator) to refresh the cache after fixing your tags.
