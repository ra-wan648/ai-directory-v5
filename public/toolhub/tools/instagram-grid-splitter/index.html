Instagram Grid Splitter

Upload any image and split it into a seamless Instagram grid — 3×3, 3×2, or 3×1. Preview the numbered tiles and download each one as a PNG.

How to use

  1. Pick a JPG or PNG from your device — it never leaves your browser.
  2. Choose a grid size: 3×3 (9 tiles), 3×2 (6 tiles), or 3×1 (3 tiles).
  3. Preview the numbered tiles and download each as tile-1.png, tile-2.png, and so on — or hit Download all tiles.

Tile quality

Each tile is exported as a full-quality PNG at one third of your image's width. For crisp results on Instagram (1080 px per tile), upload an image at least 3240 px wide for a 3×3 grid.

Posting order

Tiles are numbered top-left to bottom-right, but Instagram profiles display the newest post at the bottom-left. Post the tiles starting from the highest number to rebuild the image correctly on your profile.

Frequently asked questions

What size are the tiles?

Each tile is one third of your image's width and one row of its height, exported as a full-quality PNG. For crisp results on Instagram, upload an image at least 3240 px wide for a 3×3 grid (1080 px per tile).

How are the tiles numbered?

Tiles are numbered left to right, top to bottom — tile 1 is the top-left corner. Note that Instagram profiles display the bottom row first, so post tiles starting from the highest number to assemble the grid correctly.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Splitting happens entirely in your browser with the canvas API; your image never leaves your device.

Is this Instagram Grid Splitter free?

Yes, it is completely free, runs entirely in your browser, and requires no signup.
