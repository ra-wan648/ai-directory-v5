Spin the Wheel

Spin a colorful prize wheel to pick randomly.

Press SPIN to pick a winner.

How to use

  1. List your options — Type one option per line — names, prizes, dinner ideas, anything. The wheel redraws as you edit.
  2. Spin the wheel — Hit SPIN and watch it whirl with a smooth slow-down. The pointer at the top picks the winner.
  3. Announce the winner — The winner is revealed with a confetti burst. Tick “remove winner” to spin again without repeats.

What is Spin the Wheel?

Spin the Wheel is a colorful random picker for decisions, raffles, classroom picks and party games. Type your options, spin, and let the wheel decide — no arguments about who goes first, what to eat, or who won the giveaway.

How this fun tool works

Each option becomes a colored segment on a canvas wheel. Spinning picks a random final angle and animates the slow-down with an easing curve, so the result is genuinely unpredictable. Everything runs in your browser with plain JavaScript randomness, and the optional confetti burst is drawn on a second canvas — no libraries, no uploads.

Spin the Wheel — frequently asked questions

Is Spin the Wheel free to use?

Yes. Spin as many times as you like, free, with no signup.

Is the result really random?

Yes. Each spin chooses a random stopping angle using your browser's random number generator, so every option has an equal chance.

How many options can I add?

Up to 24 — enough for big raffles while keeping every label readable.

Does anything leave my browser?

No. Your options and spins stay on your device.
