    How to use

    1. Set your daily goal in milliliters (2000 ml is the default) and press Save goal.
    2. Every time you drink water, tap a quick-add button or type a custom amount and press Add.
    3. Watch the progress bar and percentage fill up; your 7-day history appears below and resets automatically at midnight.

    Why track water intake

    Most people underestimate how little they drink until they see the numbers. A visible daily target with one-tap logging turns hydration into a small game you can win — and the 7-day chart makes streaks obvious, which is the strongest motivator for keeping the habit.

    How your data is stored

    Intake is saved per calendar day in your browser's localStorage, so yesterday's total never leaks into today. Nothing leaves your device, and there is no account to create or sync to manage.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much water should I drink per day?

    A common guideline is around 2000 ml (about 8 glasses) for an average adult, but needs vary with body size, activity, and climate. The tracker lets you set any goal that suits you — consider asking a health professional for personal advice.

    Does the tracker reset automatically?

    Yes. Intake is stored per calendar day, so a new day starts at zero automatically at midnight while your previous days stay visible in the 7-day history.

    Is my intake data private?

    Yes. Everything is saved in your browser's localStorage on this device only — nothing is uploaded or shared.

    Is this Water Intake Tracker free?

    Yes, it is completely free, runs entirely in your browser, and requires no signup.
