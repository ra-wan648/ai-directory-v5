Tweet Screenshot Maker

Design a realistic tweet mockup — display name, handle, 280-character text, engagement stats, verified badge, dark or light theme — and export it as a crisp PNG.

280 characters left

How to use

  1. Fill in the tweet details: display name, handle, text (up to 280 characters), time, and engagement stats.
  2. Style it: pick the dark or light theme and toggle the verified badge on or off.
  3. Check the live preview, then hit Download PNG to save a 2x-resolution image.

How the PNG export works

The styled preview is serialized into an SVG with an embedded foreignObject, drawn onto a canvas at 2x resolution, and exported with canvas.toDataURL — no external libraries. It works in Chrome, Edge, and Firefox.

Made for mockups

This tool creates design mockups and concept screenshots for presentations, portfolios, and product pitches. It is not affiliated with X/Twitter.

Frequently asked questions

How is the PNG generated?

The preview is serialized into an SVG with an embedded foreignObject, drawn onto a canvas at 2x resolution, and exported with canvas.toDataURL — no external libraries are used. It works in Chrome, Edge, and Firefox.

What is the character limit?

Tweet text is limited to 280 characters with a live counter, matching the real platform limit.

Can I add the verified badge?

Yes — toggle the verified badge checkbox to show the blue checkmark next to the display name, or hide it for an unverified look.

Is this Tweet Screenshot Maker free?

Yes, it is completely free, runs entirely in your browser, and requires no signup.
