How to use Quote Generator

  1. Fill in the parties — Enter your business name, the client name, a quote number, the date and the valid-until date.

  3. Print, save or copy — Hit Print / Save PDF to download a clean quote document, or Copy summary to paste the totals into an email.

What is Quote Generator?

The Quote Generator builds polished estimates in seconds: add line items, apply tax, set an expiry date and terms, and watch an exact live preview update as you type.

How this social & business tool works

Everything is computed instantly with plain JavaScript. The Print / Save PDF button opens your browser's print dialog with a dedicated print stylesheet, so the document — and only the document — becomes the PDF. The Copy summary button puts a plain-text breakdown on your clipboard for emails.

Privacy and limits

All data lives in the page's memory only. Nothing is uploaded or stored — keep the tab open while you work, or save the PDF to keep your copy.

Quote Generator — frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a quote and an invoice?

A quote (estimate) is sent before work begins to propose a price; it usually carries a valid-until date. An invoice is sent after work is done to request payment. This tool builds quotes — for invoices, use our free Invoice Generator.

How do I save the quote as a PDF?

Press “Print / Save PDF” and choose “Save as PDF” in your browser's print dialog. The print stylesheet formats only the quote document — no buttons, no page chrome.

Is the quote generator free?

Yes — free and unlimited, no account and no signup. Everything is generated locally in your browser.

Is my quote data uploaded anywhere?

No. The preview and the PDF are built entirely on your device with JavaScript. Nothing is stored or sent to any server.
