What is Base Converter?

Base Converter translates integers between any bases from 2 to 36 — binary, octal, decimal, hexadecimal and everything in between — with mathematically exact results.

How this developer tool works

Your input is validated digit-by-digit against the source base, accumulated into a BigInt, then repeatedly divided into the target base. The quick row renders base 2, 8, 10 and 16 from the same value.

Privacy and limits

All conversion happens locally in your browser. Only integers are supported (no fractions); extremely long inputs (thousands of digits) may take a moment.

Base Converter — frequently asked questions

Why does this converter use BigInt?

Normal JavaScript numbers lose precision past 2^53 (about 9 quadrillion). BigInt represents integers of any size exactly, so even 200-digit conversions are correct.

Which bases are supported?

Every integer base from 2 (binary) to 36 (digits 0–9 plus letters a–z). Bases above 16 are handy for compact encodings.

Why do I get an “invalid digit” error?

A digit is only legal if it is smaller than the source base: binary accepts only 0 and 1, hex accepts 0–9 and a–f. The error names the offending character and its position so you can fix it fast.

Can it convert negative numbers?

Yes — a leading minus sign is accepted and carried through to the result and the quick-conversion row.
