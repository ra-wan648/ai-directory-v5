Why 1 to 3999?

Standard Roman numerals have no zero and no symbol above M (1000), so the largest value representable in standard form is 3999 — MMMCMXCIX. Larger values need the non-standard vinculum (overline) notation.

Subtractive notation

A smaller numeral before a larger one is subtracted, but only six pairs are valid: IV (4), IX (9), XL (40), XC (90), CD (400), and CM (900). Anything else — like IL or XM — is rejected as invalid.

Frequently asked questions

Why only 1 to 3999?

Standard Roman numerals have no zero and no symbols beyond M (1000). The largest representable value in standard form is 3999 (MMMCMXCIX). Larger values need non-standard vinculum notation.

What makes a Roman numeral invalid?

Repeating V, L, or D, repeating any symbol more than three times (e.g. IIII), invalid subtractive pairs (e.g. IL), or wrong ordering (e.g. XM) are all rejected as invalid.

How does subtractive notation work?

A smaller numeral before a larger one is subtracted: IV = 4, IX = 9, XL = 40, XC = 90, CD = 400, CM = 900. Only these six pairs are valid.
