How to use Collage Maker

  1. Add your photos — Upload 2 to 9 images from your device. Reorder them with the arrow buttons or click one to replace it.
  2. Pick a layout and style — Choose one of 5 layouts, then tune the gap size, background color and rounded corners.
  3. Download the collage — Hit Download PNG or JPG to save the finished collage. Your photos never leave your browser.

What is Collage Maker?

Collage Maker arranges 2–9 photos into a single share-ready image: a classic 2×2 or 3×3 grid, a horizontal or vertical strip, or a "featured" layout with one large photo beside smaller ones — ideal for social posts, mood boards and before/after shots.

How this image tool works

Your photos are drawn onto an HTML canvas with cover-fit cropping, so every cell is filled edge to edge. Gap, background color and corner radius are applied live, and the download exports the exact canvas at full resolution.

Privacy and limits

All compositing happens in your browser — photos are never uploaded or stored. Very large source images are downscaled to the collage canvas size automatically, keeping the download fast.

Collage Maker — frequently asked questions

Is the collage maker free?

Yes. Collage Maker on AI Directory is completely free — no account, no watermark on your collage, and no limits.

How many photos can I combine?

Between 2 and 9 photos. Grid layouts work best with 4, 6 or 9 photos; the strip layouts handle any count in between.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your photos are composited on a canvas entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

How do I change the order of photos?

Use the ↑ and ↓ arrow buttons under each thumbnail to move it, click a thumbnail to replace that photo, or use ✕ to remove one. The collage updates instantly.
