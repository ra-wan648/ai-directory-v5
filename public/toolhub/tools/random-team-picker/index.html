Random Team Picker

Shuffle names into fair random teams.

How to use

  1. Paste the names — One name per line — a class list, players, colleagues, anyone.
  2. Choose teams or team size — Split into a fixed number of teams, or set a team size and let the tool figure out the count.
  3. Shuffle fairly — Hit Shuffle for a Fisher-Yates random draw, dealt round-robin so uneven splits differ by at most one.

What is the Random Team Picker?

The Random Team Picker shuffles a list of names into fair, random teams — perfect for PE classes, office games, hackathons and trivia nights. Paste the names, choose how many teams (or how big each team should be), and get instant team cards you can reshuffle with one click.

How this fun tool works

Names are shuffled with the Fisher-Yates algorithm — the standard unbiased shuffle — then dealt round-robin across teams, which is the fairest way to handle uneven splits: no team ever has more than one extra member. It all runs in your browser, so class lists and colleague names never leave your device.

Random Team Picker — frequently asked questions

Is the Random Team Picker free to use?

Yes. Shuffle unlimited lists, free, with no signup.

How are names shuffled?

With the Fisher-Yates shuffle, the standard algorithm for an unbiased random ordering.

What happens with uneven splits?

Names are dealt round-robin, so teams differ by at most one member — the fairest possible split.

Are the names I paste uploaded?

No. The list is shuffled entirely in your browser and disappears when you leave the page.
