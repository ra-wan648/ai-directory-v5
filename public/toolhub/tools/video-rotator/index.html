Video Rotator

Rotate videos 90° or 180°, or flip them horizontally or vertically. The whole conversion runs inside your browser with FFmpeg — your video is never uploaded anywhere.

How to use

  1. Pick a video file — choose an MP4, WebM or MOV file from your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. Choose the rotation or flip — pick 90° clockwise, 90° counter-clockwise, 180°, horizontal flip or vertical flip.
  3. Apply and download — click Rotate video. The engine applies the transform with the audio track untouched, then your download starts automatically.

Rotation vs flip

Rotation turns the picture itself: 90° fixes sideways phone recordings, 180° fixes upside-down ones. A flip mirrors the picture instead — horizontal flip creates a mirror image (handy for correcting flipped selfie-camera footage), while vertical flip turns the picture top-to-bottom.

Fixing sideways phone videos

Videos recorded with the phone held sideways are the classic case: choose Rotate 90° clockwise or counter-clockwise and the video plays upright everywhere. The audio track is copied without re-encoding, so sound quality is identical.

Frequently asked questions

Is the video rotator free?

Yes. The Video Rotator on AI Directory is completely free — no account, no watermark, and no limit on how many videos you rotate.

Does my video get uploaded to a server?

No. The FFmpeg engine runs entirely in your browser via WebAssembly. Your video file never leaves your device.

Will rotating reduce the video quality?

The video is re-encoded at high quality (H.264, CRF 23), so any quality change is visually negligible. The audio track is copied untouched, with zero quality change.

What is the difference between rotate and flip?

Rotation turns the picture (90° or 180°), fixing sideways recordings. Flip mirrors the picture: horizontal flip creates a mirror image, vertical flip turns it upside down without rotating.
