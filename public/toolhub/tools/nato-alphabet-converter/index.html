The NATO phonetic alphabet

  
The ICAO/NATO spelling alphabet assigns an unambiguous word to every letter: Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta, Echo, Foxtrot, Golf, Hotel, India, Juliett, Kilo, Lima, Mike, November, Oscar, Papa, Quebec, Romeo, Sierra, Tango, Uniform, Victor, Whiskey, X-ray, Yankee, Zulu. Pilots, military radio operators, and support teams use it to spell names and codes over noisy channels.

  
How decoding works

  
When converting NATO words back to text, the tool matches words case-insensitively and handles multi-word tokens like "Question mark" automatically. Anything in [brackets] was not recognized — check the spelling.






  
Frequently asked questions

  
Which NATO words are used?
The standard ICAO/NATO alphabet: Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, Delta, Echo, Foxtrot, Golf, Hotel, India, Juliett, Kilo, Lima, Mike, November, Oscar, Papa, Quebec, Romeo, Sierra, Tango, Uniform, Victor, Whiskey, X-ray, Yankee, Zulu.

  
Are digits and punctuation supported?
Yes. Digits map to Zero through Nine, and common punctuation (period, comma, question mark, exclamation, @, dash, slash, and more) maps to spoken words like Dot, Comma, and Question mark. Decoding handles multi-word tokens such as Question mark automatically.

  
What does [brackets] in the output mean?
A token in brackets was not recognized — either an unsupported character (forward) or a word that is not in the NATO alphabet (reverse). Check the spelling and try again.

  
Is this NATO Alphabet Converter free?
Yes, it is completely free, runs entirely in your browser, and requires no signup.
