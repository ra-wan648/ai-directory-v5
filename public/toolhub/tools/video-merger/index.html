Video Merger

Join two or more video files into one seamless MP4, re-encoded for compatibility. The whole merge runs inside your browser with FFmpeg — your videos are never uploaded anywhere.

Tip: hold Ctrl (or Cmd on Mac) to select several files at once. Your files never leave your device.

    0%

    The video engine (~30 MB) loads only when you press the button — once. It is cached in your browser after the first run, so later uses start instantly.

    How to use

    1. Select your videos — choose two or more MP4, WebM or MOV files from your device, in the order you want them joined. Nothing is uploaded.
    2. Merge — click Merge videos. The FFmpeg engine concatenates the clips and re-encodes them into one compatible MP4.
    3. Download the result — your merged MP4 is ready; the download starts automatically and a link stays on the page.

    How the merge works

    The tool uses FFmpeg's concat demuxer to join your clips end to end, then re-encodes everything to H.264 video and AAC audio (the libx264/aac codecs). Re-encoding — instead of a raw stream copy — is what makes the merge reliable even when the input files use different codecs or settings.

    Order matters

    Clips are joined in the exact order they appear in the file picker. The output resolution follows the first clip, so for the cleanest result use clips that share the same resolution and orientation.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is the video merger free?

    Yes. The Video Merger on AI Directory is completely free — no account, no watermark, and no limit on how many times you merge.

    Do my videos get uploaded to a server?

    No. The FFmpeg engine runs entirely in your browser via WebAssembly. Your video files never leave your device.

    What if my videos have different resolutions or codecs?

    The tool re-encodes every clip to H.264 video and AAC audio, so inputs with different codecs join reliably. For the smoothest result, use clips with the same resolution; the output takes the first clip's resolution.

    How many videos can I merge at once?

    There is no hard limit, but every clip is held in browser memory during the merge. Merging many large files can exhaust memory — if it fails, try fewer or smaller clips.
