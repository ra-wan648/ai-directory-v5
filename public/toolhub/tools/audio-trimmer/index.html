How to use Audio Trimmer

  1. Upload an audio file — Choose an MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A or other audio file. It is decoded locally in your browser — never uploaded.
  2. Select the region to trim — Drag across the waveform to select a region, or type exact start/end times. Preview it before you commit.
  3. Trim and download — Choose "Keep selection" to export only that part, or "Remove selection" to cut it out — then download the WAV.

What is Audio Trimmer?

Audio Trimmer cuts a clip out of an audio file — or cuts a section out — without installing an editor. Upload any common audio format, mark the region on a visual waveform, and export a clean WAV you can use anywhere.

How this audio tool works

Your file is decoded with the browser's native Web Audio API into raw samples. Trimming slices those samples precisely at your selection, and a built-in WAV encoder writes the result as uncompressed 16-bit PCM audio.

Privacy and limits

Decoding, trimming and encoding all happen on your device — your audio is never uploaded or stored. Very long files (30+ minutes) can use significant memory; work with shorter clips for best results.

Audio Trimmer — frequently asked questions

Is the audio trimmer free?

Yes. Audio Trimmer on AI Directory is completely free — no account, no watermarks, no limits.

Is my audio uploaded to a server?

No. Your file is decoded with the browser's Web Audio API and the trimmed result is encoded to WAV right on your device. Nothing is ever uploaded.

What formats can I upload, and what do I get back?

You can upload any format your browser can play — MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, FLAC and more. The trimmed result downloads as an uncompressed WAV file, which plays everywhere.

What's the difference between "Keep selection" and "Remove selection"?

"Keep selection" exports only the highlighted region — ideal for cutting a clip out of a longer recording. "Remove selection" cuts the highlighted part out and joins the rest — ideal for deleting coughs, silences or mistakes.
