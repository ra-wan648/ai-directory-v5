  1. Enter your target income — Type the annual take-home salary you want, plus business expenses and tax rate.
  2. Set your billable time — Enter realistic billable hours per week and working weeks per year.
  3. Quote projects — Use your hourly rate in the project estimator to price any job by hours.

What is Freelance Rate Calculator?

The Freelance Rate Calculator converts the salary you want into the hourly rate you must charge, grossing up for taxes and adding business expenses, spread over only the hours you can actually bill.

How this finance tool works

Your take-home target is grossed up for tax (salary ÷ (1 − tax rate)), annual expenses are added, and the total is divided by yearly billable hours (hours/week × weeks/year). The day rate multiplies the hourly rate by your average billable day.

Privacy and limits

All math runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored. Results are planning estimates, not tax advice.

Freelance Rate Calculator — frequently asked questions

How is the hourly rate calculated?

First your take-home target is grossed up for tax (salary / (1 - tax rate)), then annual business expenses are added. That total is divided by your yearly billable hours (hours/week x weeks/year). Example: $60,000 target, $5,000 expenses, 20% tax, 25 hrs/week x 48 weeks gives about $66.67/hr.

Why is the freelance rate higher than an employee hourly wage?

Freelancers pay their own taxes, insurance, tools, unpaid admin time and gaps between clients. The calculator builds tax and expenses into the rate, and the billable-hours input should only count hours you can actually charge for.

What is the day rate based on?

The day rate is your hourly rate multiplied by your average billable hours per day (weekly hours divided by 5). Adjust your weekly hours if your working days are longer or shorter.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. All calculations run in JavaScript in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.
