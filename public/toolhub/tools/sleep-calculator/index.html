How to use Sleep Calculator

  1. Pick a mode — “I want to wake at…” computes ideal bedtimes for a fixed alarm; “I'm going to bed now” computes ideal wake-up times from this moment.
  2. Enter the time — Type your wake-up time or your planned bedtime — the 90-minute cycle math updates instantly.
  3. Choose a cycle — Pick 6 cycles (~9 h) for a full night, 5 (~7.5 h) for a standard night, or 4 (~6 h) when time is short.

What is Sleep Calculator?

The Sleep Calculator plans your bedtime or alarm around 90-minute sleep cycles, so you wake at the light end of a cycle instead of being dragged out of deep sleep.

How this health tool works

Wake-time mode subtracts 15 minutes of falling-asleep time plus 6, 5 or 4 × 90-minute cycles from your alarm. Bedtime mode adds them forward. Everything is computed instantly in your browser with plain date math.

Privacy and limits

Nothing is stored or uploaded — the times you enter never leave your device. This is general sleep-hygiene information, not medical advice.

Sleep Calculator — frequently asked questions

Why 90-minute sleep cycles?

A full sleep cycle — light sleep, deep sleep and REM — lasts roughly 90 minutes for most adults. Waking at the end of a cycle (rather than mid-cycle) is what makes you feel refreshed instead of groggy.

Why do you subtract 15 minutes from bedtime?

Most people need about 15 minutes to fall asleep after getting into bed. The calculator assumes you fall asleep 15 minutes after your bedtime so the cycles line up with real sleep, not lights-out.

How many sleep cycles do I need?

Most adults do well on 5 cycles (~7.5 hours of sleep). 6 cycles (~9 hours) suits heavy training or sleep debt; 4 cycles (~6 hours) is a workable minimum for a single night but not a long-term habit.

Is my data saved?

No. Times are computed in your browser and nothing is stored or uploaded. This tool is general information, not medical advice.
