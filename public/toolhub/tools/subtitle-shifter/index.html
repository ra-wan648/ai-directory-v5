Subtitle Shifter

Shift SRT subtitle timing forward or backward.

How to use Subtitle Shifter

  1. Paste your SRT text into the box or upload an .srt file.
  2. Enter the offset in milliseconds — negative values shift subtitles earlier, positive values later.
  3. Click "Shift subtitles" to preview the result, then download the new .srt file.

What is Subtitle Shifter?

Subtitle Shifter is a free tool that adjusts the timing of SRT subtitle files. If your subtitles appear too early or too late in a video, you can move every cue forward or backward by an exact number of milliseconds until they are in sync.

How it works

The tool parses each SRT cue's timestamps (HH:MM:SS,mmm), adds your offset to both the start and end times, and rebuilds the file. Any timestamp that would go below zero is clamped to 00:00:00,000. The subtitle text itself is never changed.

Privacy

All processing happens locally on your device. Your subtitles never leave your browser and nothing is uploaded to any server.

FAQ

Is Subtitle Shifter free?

Yes. It is completely free with no limits and no hidden costs.

Do I need an account?

No. No signup or account is required — everything runs directly in your browser.

Is my subtitle file uploaded to a server?

No. Your subtitles are processed entirely on your device with JavaScript. No file ever leaves your browser.

What is Subtitle Shifter best used for?

It is best for fixing subtitles that are out of sync with the video — shifting them earlier or later by a precise number of milliseconds.
