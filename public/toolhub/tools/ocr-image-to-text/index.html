How to use Image to Text (OCR)

  1. Choose an image — Drag and drop a PNG, JPG or WebP image onto the drop zone, or click it to browse your files.
  2. Extract the text — Click Extract Text. The OCR engine runs entirely in your browser; the first run downloads the engine (~30MB) and caches it for later.
  3. Copy or download — Copy the recognized text to your clipboard or download it as a .txt file. Your image never leaves your device.

What is Image to Text (OCR)?

Image to Text turns pictures of text into editable text you can copy and use: screenshots, scanned pages, photos of signs, receipts and whiteboards. It uses the Tesseract OCR engine running directly in your browser.

How this image tool works

The Tesseract.js library loads once on your first extraction (about 30MB, cached by the browser afterwards). When you click Extract Text, a worker is created, the image is recognized locally, and the worker is shut down again to free memory.

Privacy and limits

Recognition happens 100% on your device — no image is ever uploaded. Recognition is fixed to English and works best on clear, high-contrast text; handwritten or heavily stylized text may give partial results.

Image to Text (OCR) — frequently asked questions

Is the image-to-text OCR tool free?

Yes. The Image to Text tool on AI Directory is completely free — no account, no watermark and no limit on how many images you process.

Which languages can it recognize?

This tool is fixed to English recognition. It works best on clear, high-contrast text such as documents, screenshots, receipts and signs.

Does my image leave my browser?

No. The OCR engine (Tesseract.js) runs entirely on your device. Your image is never uploaded to any server.

Why does the first run take longer?

The first extraction downloads the OCR engine (~30MB) and the English language data. They are cached by your browser, so later runs start instantly.
