What is Calorie Calculator?

The Calorie Calculator estimates how many calories your body burns per day using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — the most validated BMR formula — then adjusts for your activity level and goal.

How this health tool works

BMR is computed from age, sex, height and weight; multiplying by your activity factor gives TDEE. Goal presets add or subtract calories, and macros follow common evidence-based anchors: 2.0 g protein and 0.9 g fat per kg of body weight, with remaining calories as carbs. All math runs in your browser.

Calorie Calculator — frequently asked questions

What is the Mifflin-St Jeor formula?

The most widely validated BMR equation: for men, 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) − 5 × age + 5; for women the same with −161 instead of +5. TDEE is BMR multiplied by an activity factor.

How are the macros calculated?

Protein is set to 2.0 g per kg of body weight and fat to 0.9 g per kg; remaining calories go to carbs (4 kcal/g). These are common evidence-based starting points, not medical advice.

How fast will I lose or gain weight?

A 500 kcal daily deficit typically means about 0.5 kg (1 lb) of fat loss per week; a 300 kcal surplus is a lean-gain pace. Real results vary — track your weight for 2–3 weeks and adjust.

Is my data saved or shared?

No. Your age, weight and other inputs never leave your browser — nothing is stored, uploaded or shared. This tool is for general information only and is not medical advice.
