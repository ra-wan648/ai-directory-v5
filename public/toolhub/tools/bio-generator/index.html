Bio Generator

Generate polished bios for LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram and resumes.

Fill in the form and click Generate bios.

How to use

  1. Enter your details — Type your name, current role, years of experience and a few skills. These feed every platform variant.
  2. Pick a tone — Choose professional, friendly or bold to change how the bio sounds — from boardroom-safe to scroll-stopping.
  3. Copy the variant you need — Each of the four bios shows its character count, with a copy button sized to the platform's limits.

What is the Bio Generator?

The Bio Generator writes polished short bios for the four places people check first: LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram and your resume. Give it your role, experience and skills, pick a tone, and it drafts platform-ready copy that respects each network's character limits — 160 for Twitter, 150 for Instagram — so you can paste and post.

How this social & business tool works

The generator uses hand-written templates tuned per platform and per tone, filled with your details and trimmed to fit the limits. Nothing is sent anywhere: the draft is composed in your browser from the text you typed, which makes it a safe starting point you can edit freely before publishing.

Bio Generator — frequently asked questions

Is the Bio Generator free to use?

Yes. Generate as many bios as you like, free, with no signup and no saved drafts.

Does it respect character limits?

Yes. Twitter bios are capped at 160 characters and Instagram at 150 — each variant shows its live character count.

Can I edit the generated bio?

Absolutely — treat it as a strong first draft. Tweak the wording so it sounds like you before you publish.

Is anything I type uploaded?

No. Your name, role and skills stay in your browser; the bio is assembled locally from templates.
