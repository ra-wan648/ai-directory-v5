What is Video Compressor?

The Video Compressor re-encodes your MP4 with the H.264 codec at a quality level you choose — High keeps near-original quality, Medium balances size and looks, Low makes the smallest file. It shows the input vs output size and the percentage saved.

How this video tool works

This tool runs real FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly in your browser. When you press compress, it executes the equivalent of ffmpeg -i input -vcodec libx264 -crf 23/28/33 -preset veryfast -acodec aac output.mp4 — High uses CRF 23, Medium CRF 28, Low CRF 33.

Privacy and limits

Your video never leaves your device: the FFmpeg engine downloads (~30 MB, cached after first use) and runs locally. Re-encoding is CPU-heavy, so large videos take a few minutes — keep the tab open. Files over ~500 MB may be very slow in a browser.

Video Compressor — frequently asked questions

Is the video compressor free?

Yes. The Video Compressor on AI Directory is completely free — no account, no watermark, and no limit on how many videos you compress.

How much smaller will my video get?

It depends on the source. The Medium setting typically cuts 50–80% off phone or screen recordings. Already heavily compressed videos shrink less.

Will compression reduce the quality?

High (CRF 23) is visually near-identical to the original for most videos. Medium (CRF 28) shows only minor softening. Low (CRF 33) is visibly softer but much smaller — good for drafts or previews.

Does my video get uploaded to a server?

No. The FFmpeg engine runs entirely in your browser via WebAssembly. Your video file never leaves your device.
