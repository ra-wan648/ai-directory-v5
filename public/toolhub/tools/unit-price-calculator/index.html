How to use Unit Price Calculator

  1. Add your products — Enter a name, price, quantity and unit for each item you want to compare.
  2. See price per unit — Each row shows its price per unit instantly, and the cheapest is marked Best value.
  3. Sort by value — Hit Sort by best value to rank the list from cheapest to priciest per unit.

What is Unit Price Calculator?

The Unit Price Calculator normalises product prices to a single comparable figure — price ÷ quantity — so you can spot the genuinely cheaper pack instead of guessing from shelf prices.

How this utilitie tool works

Every row is recomputed on each keystroke, the lowest price-per-unit is highlighted with a Best value badge, and one click sorts the table from best to worst value.

Privacy and limits

It is a pure browser calculation: no account, no saved history. Use matching units across rows for a fair comparison.

Unit Price Calculator — frequently asked questions

How is the price per unit calculated?

Simple division: price ÷ quantity. A 470 pack of 10 kg rice is 47.00 per kg — cheaper than a 250 pack of 5 kg at 50.00 per kg.

Can I compare different pack sizes?

That is exactly what it is for. Enter each pack's price and quantity; the calculator normalises them to one comparable figure.

Do the units need to match across rows?

Each row computes with its own unit label. For a fair comparison, use the same unit (e.g. all per kg) for the products you want to rank.

Is my shopping list saved anywhere?

No. Rows exist only in the page; closing the tab clears them.
