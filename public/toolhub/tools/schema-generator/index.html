Schema Markup Generator

Generate JSON-LD schema markup for rich results. Free, no signup, and everything runs in your browser.

How to use Schema Markup Generator

  1. Pick a schema type — Choose Article, FAQPage, Product, LocalBusiness, BreadcrumbList or Organization.
  2. Fill in the fields — Complete the form — required fields are marked, and FAQ/breadcrumb rows can be added or removed.
  3. Generate and copy — Hit Generate to get formatted JSON-LD, check the validation message, then copy and paste it into your page's <head>.

What is the Schema Markup Generator?

Structured data in JSON-LD format helps Google understand your page and can unlock rich results like FAQ dropdowns, product prices, review stars and breadcrumbs. Pick one of six common schema types, fill the form, and get copy-ready markup.

How do I install the markup?

Copy the generated script and paste it inside the of the page it describes. Then test it with Google's Rich Results Test to confirm eligibility — markup alone doesn't guarantee a rich result, but without it you aren't eligible.

Which schema type should I use?

Articles and blog posts use Article; pages with Q&A content use FAQPage; shops use Product; physical locations use LocalBusiness; every business site can add Organization; and BreadcrumbList helps Google show breadcrumb trails in search results.

Schema Markup Generator — frequently asked questions

Is the Schema Markup Generator free?

Yes — free with no signup and no limits.

Is the output validated?

Yes. Every generation is parsed back with JSON.parse and checked for required fields before you copy it.

Does schema markup guarantee rich results?

No. Valid markup makes you eligible, but Google decides whether to show a rich result. Follow Google's content guidelines for each type.

Where do I paste the JSON-LD?

Inside a <script type="application/ld+json"> tag in your page's <head>, on the page the schema describes.
